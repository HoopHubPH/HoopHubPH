1. Select what is wrongChoose the finalized game, correction type, and player/stat if needed.
2. Compare the valuesEnter what HoopHub currently shows, then enter what you believe the correct value should be.
3. Explain whyGive a short reason so the commissioner knows exactly what to review.
Example: HoopHub shows 12 points, but the player should have 14 points. Enter 12 under “What HoopHub Currently Shows” and 14 under “What It Should Be.”
